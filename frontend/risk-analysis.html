Analytical Support Policy: This dashboard provides analytical indicators calculated from measurable engagement and assessment metrics (e.g. low quiz scores, declining performance trends, prolonged inactivity). It is designed strictly as a supportive faculty intervention tool and must not be interpreted as a deterministic prediction about a student's future capabilities.
1. Low Scores

Diagnostic or module quiz score below 60% threshold.

2. Declining Trend

Downwards slope across 3 consecutive assessment sessions.

3. Low Completion

Milestone pace <40% of scheduled syllabus timeline.

4. Inactivity

Zero code execution or portal visits for >7 days.
